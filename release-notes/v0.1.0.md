The first stable release of WeatherCard: a clean, offline-friendly weather app for Android, powered by the free Open-Meteo API. No account, no API key, no ads.

## Install

Download **weathercard-0.1.0.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from unknown sources.
It is signed with the same key as the nightly builds, so it installs over an existing nightly and keeps your saved cities and forecasts.

## What's in it

- Big, readable current conditions on a full-screen sky backdrop.
- A scrollable hourly temperature curve, a 7-day forecast, and wind, sun, humidity, feels-like, UV, pressure and rain details.
- Search for cities, save up to 10 and swipe between them; your current location can be the first page.
- A home-screen widget that shows the selected place from the saved forecast.
- Works offline: forecasts are stored per location and a failed refresh never deletes saved data.
- English and Turkish, following your phone's language.

## New since the last nightly

- **Hourly curve starts now**: past hours are hidden and the curve begins at "Now". While the app is on screen it refreshes the next 6 hours once every hour, and never in the background.
- **Home-screen widget** showing the selected city from the saved forecast, redrawn every hour.
- **Several cities**: save up to 10 places and swipe between them on the main screen.
- **Animated backdrop** that follows the weather and the time of day, with sunrise and sunset tints based on each city's own sun times. It stays still when system animations are turned off.
- **English and Turkish**: the app follows the system language (on Android 13+ you can pick a language just for WeatherCard).
- WeatherCard is now open source under the GNU GPL v3.0.
