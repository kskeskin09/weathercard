WeatherCard 0.2.0 makes the forecast easier to explore and keeps it fresh on its own.

## Install

Download **weathercard-0.2.0.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from unknown sources.
It is signed with the same key as 0.1.0 and the nightly builds, so it installs over them and keeps your saved cities and forecasts.

## What's new

- **Tap a day for its hours**: tapping a row in the 7-day forecast expands it to show that day's 24 hours from the saved forecast.
- **Always fresh while open**: when you open the app, come back to it or swipe to another city, the full 7-day forecast for the visible city is refreshed if it is more than an hour old. Nothing runs in the background, and after an error the saved forecast is kept and the app waits an hour before trying again.
- **One place to refresh everything**: "Refresh all" in the ⋮ menu updates every saved city at once. Pull to refresh still updates the next 6 hours. The separate refresh buttons on the cards are gone.
- **Search as you type**: city results appear while you type, without a search button.
- **Settings**: a new Settings page in the ⋮ menu lets you turn the location page off (your saved cities stay). A fresh start opens on the location page.
- **Calmer look**: the animated background fades behind the cards with a muted glass fill, the hourly header sits inside its card, the 7-day list always shows all seven days, and each hourly date shows its short weekday.

## Fixes

- Swiping between cities works when the swipe starts on the hourly chart.
- Day labels such as "Tomorrow" stay on one line.
